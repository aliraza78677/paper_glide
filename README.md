# Paper Glide

A small browser game. Tap, click or press Space to keep a paper plane in the air and fly through 15 towers. The last 5 are a little harder. Clear all 15 to win.

Made with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No libraries or build step.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any browser, or visit the GitHub Pages link once it is turned on.

## Controls

- Tap, click, Space or Up arrow: lift
- Sound button under the game: turn sound on or off
